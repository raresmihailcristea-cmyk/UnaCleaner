# UnaCleaner

**Native Storage Cleaner, AI Photo Declutterer, Contacts Organizer & System Optimizer for macOS, iPhone & iPad**

Engineered natively in Swift 6 and SwiftUI with a 100% on-device, zero-telemetry architecture strictly compliant with Apple's App Sandbox.

## Key Features

- **One-Tap Smart Scan**: Unified diagnostic scan across redundant caches, duplicate files, oversized videos, and reclaimable storage.
- **AI Photos & Video Cleaner**: On-device Apple Vision Best Shot detection, Swipe-to-Clean deck, HEVC 720p video compression, and Live Photo to still conversion.
- **Contacts Cleaner**: Instant duplicate contact detection and merging with one-tap local vCard (`.vcf`) safety backups.
- **Calendar Date Filtering**: Browse and filter scanned items by Today, 7 Days, 30 Days, >90 Days, or custom calendar dates across modules.
- **100% Local Privacy**: Zero cloud uploads, zero analytics SDKs, and zero third-party tracking.
